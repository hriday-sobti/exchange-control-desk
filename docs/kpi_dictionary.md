# KPI Dictionary & Formulations: Exchange Control Desk

This document specifies the exact business definitions, mathematical formulas, data sources, and grains for all key performance indicators tracked across **Exchange Control Desk**.

---

## 1. Executive & Platform KPIs

### Total Transactions
- **Definition:** The total count of all discrete transactional events (deposits, withdrawals, trades) recorded on the platform.
- **Formula:** $\sum 1$ where `transaction_id IS NOT NULL`
- **Data Source:** `fact_transactions`
- **Grain:** Aggregate platform level / Daily date grain.

### Gross Transaction Value (GTV)
- **Definition:** The gross nominal economic value in INR of all transactions executed or attempted.
- **Formula:** $\sum \text{gross\_value}$
- **Data Source:** `fact_transactions.gross_value`
- **Grain:** Currency in INR.

### Active Users (DAU / MAU)
- **Definition:** Count of distinct user accounts that executed at least one transaction within the designated time window (Daily or Monthly).
- **Formula:** $\text{COUNT(DISTINCT } \text{user\_id)}$
- **Data Source:** `fact_transactions.user_id`

### Platform Success Rate
- **Definition:** The proportion of attempted platform transactions that successfully reached terminal `COMPLETED` status.
- **Formula:** $\frac{\sum \mathbb{I}(\text{status} = \text{'COMPLETED'})}{\text{Total Transactions}} \times 100\%$
- **Data Source:** `fact_transactions.status`

### Total Fee Revenue
- **Definition:** Total net fee revenue generated by the exchange across maker/taker trading and withdrawal processing fees.
- **Formula:** $\sum \text{fee}$ where $\text{status} = \text{'COMPLETED'}$
- **Data Source:** `fact_transactions.fee`

## 2. Risk & Operational Monitoring KPIs

### Open P1 / P2 Incidents
- **Definition:** The count of critical (P1) and high-priority (P2) operational incidents currently awaiting investigation.
- **Formula:** $\sum \mathbb{I}(\text{priority\_band} \in \{\text{'P1'}, \text{'P2'}\} \land \text{status} = \text{'OPEN'})$
- **Data Source:** `fact_incidents`

### Total Financial Exposure at Risk
- **Definition:** The cumulative INR value associated with transactions flagged in active high-severity incidents.
- **Formula:** $\sum \text{exposure\_inr}$ where $\text{priority\_band} \in \{\text{'P1'}, \text{'P2'}\}$
- **Data Source:** `fact_incidents.exposure_inr`

### Anomaly False Positive Rate (Synthetic Evaluation)
- **Definition:** The proportion of non-anomalous baseline transactions that were erroneously flagged by the detection engine.
- **Formula:** $\text{FPR} = \frac{\text{False Positives (FP)}}{\text{FP} + \text{True Negatives (TN)}}$
- **Data Source:** `outputs/anomalies/anomaly_evaluation.csv`

## 3. Data Quality Score Formulation

The automated **Data Quality Score (DQ Score)** evaluates 6 dimensions with documented weights summing to 1.00:

$$\text{DQ Score} = 100\% \times \sum_{d=1}^{6} w_{d} \times \left(1 - \text{Failure Rate}_{d}\right)$$

| Dimension ($d$) | Weight ($w_d$) | Evaluated Condition | Criticality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Completeness** | $0.25$ | Null checks on mandatory primary keys, timestamps, values | Critical |
| **Validity** | $0.20$ | Range constraints ($>0$), valid status/type enums | Critical |
| **Consistency** | $0.20$ | Balance equation: $\text{gross\_value} \approx \text{quantity} \times \text{price}$ | Medium |
| **Uniqueness** | $0.15$ | Zero duplicate transaction IDs | Critical |
| **Referential Integrity** | $0.10$ | Orphan foreign keys referencing missing users or assets | High |
| **Timeliness** | $0.10$ | Future timestamps, delayed feeds ($>24$ hours) | Medium |
